Identity · from 2a

Icon set

The court plate as the product icon at every size it ships in, and a UI family drawn on the same 48-unit grid with the same 3.5 stroke, so a nav icon and the logo look like they came from one hand.

Product icon — three detail tiers

The plate does not simply scale down. Below 32px the sideline arcs close up into mush, and below 20px the circle fills in — so detail is dropped on purpose at two thresholds rather than left to the renderer.

Tier 1 · 32px and up
Full floor: centre line, tip-off circle, both sideline arcs.
Tier 2 · 20–31px
Arcs dropped. Line and circle thicken to 4 so they hold at small sizes.
Tier 3 · under 20px
Plate and centre line only — still unmistakably a court at 16px.
